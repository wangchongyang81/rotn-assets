# cart-woman — ref pack

Realm 2 NPC. Spec only; no art yet, no anchor approved.

## Identity
Adult, guarded, watchful. Keeps the watch post above the Kharva road and knows
who passes without being told. Holds a hand up in the road, not waving, and says
only "You're the one from the cave." Does not explain how. Not a fighter; the
hands hold a strap and set a wheel. Cold, flat, unimpressed by arrival.

## Realm
Kharva, realm 2, V1 plains. Home and only realm; no crossing passes.

## Anchor
Descends from `anchor-v1-female-p` and `anchor-v1-female-t`. Neither approved
yet, so no part starts. The body never re-rolls; V1 plains comes from the
anchor, not a new letter.
