# pledge-keeper — ref pack

Worked example for 11. Spec only; no art yet, no anchor approved.

## Identity
Adult, weathered, patient. Tends the raked ring of each Pledge realm and speaks
in riddles. Wry, never hurried. Not a fighter — the hands are for raking and
setting stone, not weapons.

Appears in realms 15, 20, 25, 30. That is four realms and four variants:
V8 moon, V9 day, V3 high cold, V6 kiln.

## Open question
One body across four variants, or a variant per realm? 11 says a crossing
character keeps its original variant and only costume and palette change with
the realm. On that rule the home variant is chosen once and the other three
realms are costume and palette passes.

Proposal: home variant V8 moon (realm 15, first appearance, and the moon cut in
the bowl lip). Confirm before anchors.
