# ash-widow — ref pack

Pilot character. First pack in the set, built from an approved anchor.

## Identity
Adult, mid-thirties, unhurried. Keeps the ash ring at Dhara's cave mouth and
counts what passes the wall — thirty-one tallies, the thirty-second cut deeper.
Not a fighter; the hands count, scrape and set stone. Wry, flat, unimpressed.
Speaks in numbers.

## Realm
Dhara, realm 1, V1 plains. Home and only realm; no crossing passes.

## Anchor
Descends from `anchor-v1-female-p` and `anchor-v1-female-t`. Neither approved
yet, so no part starts. The body never re-rolls.
