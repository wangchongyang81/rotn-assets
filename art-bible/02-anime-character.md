# 02 — Anime character art

Modern Japanese VN illustration. Applies to portraits, busts, expression
sheets, CGs and gallery art.

## Line
Clean and confident. Weight varies by form: heavier on silhouette and cloth
outer edge, hairline on interior detail. Coloured line, never pure black.
Line thins on lit edges, thickens in occlusion.

## Face
Proportion locked by the character's ref pack. Eyes carry identity: layered
iris, crisp specular, one reflection high and one low. Lash mass drawn as a
solid, not hairs. Nose is a plane change and a shadow, not an outline. Mouth
volume before expression. Cheek blush as a soft radial, never a hard disc.

## Hair
Drawn in strand groups, never single strands. Specular is a band across the
crown, broken by a shadow lock. Ends taper and separate. Hair crosses the
silhouette in at least two places on three-quarter views.

## Skin
Three-tone base, warm mid, cool shadow with subsurface red at the terminator.
Sheen on nose bridge, shoulder and knee. Sweat and oil are separate passes
with their own highlight, never the base sheen reused.

## Cloth
Construction first: seams, darts, fold logic. Folds radiate from strain
points. Material decides specular — silk broad, wool suppressed, leather in a
narrow crest, metal hard-edged. Embroidery and trim sit on the form.

## Shading
Cel base with gradient overlay in the shadow only. Never gradient the lit side.
Two shadow layers maximum on skin, three on cloth. Ambient occlusion at every
contact.

## Anatomy
Ref pack decides proportion. Hands and feet get their own pass. Foreshortening
by volume, not by squash. Multi-limb and multi-contact frames get a skeleton
sketch before render.

## Expression
Per character, a sheet of at least eight, built to the same head. Brows carry
more than mouths. Ahegao is a named variant per character, not a generic one.

## Palette
From the character's sheet. Accent is one hue, used on eye and one garment
detail. Grading identical across every asset of that character.

## QC reject list
Extra or fused fingers, floating accessories, mirrored asymmetry, eye
direction not matching the scene, cloth that ignores gravity, specular on the
wrong material, colour drift from the sheet, face off the ref pack.
