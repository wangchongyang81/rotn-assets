# 00 — Direction

Binding. Later files refine this one; none may contradict it.

## Premise
Vedantic and Puranic cosmology under Holy and Dark influence, read through a
worldly, contemporary eye. Realms are places with weather and work, not dioramas.
Ethnic variety is a floor, not a garnish: every realm's people are specific.

## Two registers, one world
- Gameplay: HD-2D. Layered depth, parallax, fog, light shafts, readable ground.
  Pixel-derived sprites against modern lighting. Same perspective per region.
- Story: photorealistic, hardcore, uncensored. Real light on real skin, cloth
  with weight and weave, pores, sweat and wet. CGs are narrative moments, not
  poses on backdrops.
Both registers depict the same characters with the same faces.

## Holy and Dark
Holy reads as warm stone, gold, high sun, clean air, worn cloth.
Dark reads as cold blue-green, wet stone, low fog, bioluminescence, iron.
Neither is a filter. Light direction and material carry the meaning.

## Bodies
Anchors are nude base bodies, one per realm, rendered in the realm's own
environment in a natural pose. No T-pose, no P-pose, no paired masters.
Silhouette and proportion come from the anchor. Pose is never invented per
illustration.

## Explicitness
No ceiling. Realm key props decide the act: each realm's CG set is built around
its own prop or motif, never a generic scene. Anatomy must survive contact —
multi-limb, orifice-simultaneous and toy framing get extra passes.

## Banned
Painterly concept art, western comic inking, plastic CG doll skin, chibi outside
its own purpose, one-shot AI drift between images of one character.

## Rule of record
One uncensored explicit checkpoint per character, held fixed. The nude anchor is
the basis; every later asset derives from it. Identity is a reference pack,
never a re-roll.
