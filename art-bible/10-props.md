# 10 — Realm key props

One prop per realm, taken from that realm's own description. The prop is the
motif its CG set is built around (03). No two realms share one.

## Rule
The prop is foreground, midground or the thing acted on. It carries the realm's
Holy or Dark read, not a filter. Where both reads exist they change the prop's
light and material, not the object.

## Usage
A prop is drawn before its realm's CGs and approved on silhouette and material.
Holy and Dark variants change light direction and surface, never the object.
The prop appears in every CG of its realm, at one of the three depths.

## Realm 34
Haven, no mobs, fetch-only at tier D. Its prop is the only scene content the
realm has, so it carries the whole read: stolen light in a closed room.
