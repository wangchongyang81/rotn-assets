# 06 — QC gate

Nothing ships until it passes. A reject is not a preference.

## Gate order
1. Identity against the ref pack.
2. Anatomy, hands first.
3. Cloth and material logic.
4. Light direction matches the realm.
5. Palette matches the sheet.
6. Artifacts at 1:1.
7. Registry row exists and the name parses.

Any failure stops the asset. Reject rate is tracked per model.

## Identity
Face off the sheet is a reject, however good the image. Check: eye shape,
iris colour, lash mass, brow set, hair silhouette, body proportion, age read.
Two of seven drifting is a reject.

## Anatomy
Finger count and joint direction. Thumb on the correct side. Wrist break.
Knee and elbow that bend the wrong way. Contact points with pressure.

## Cloth
Folds from strain, not pattern. Gravity respected. Seams continuous across
the body. Specular matches material. Fastenings land on the form.

## Explicit frames
Skeleton sketch before render. Contact and insertion read without ambiguity.
Fluids follow gravity and surface. Toys keep their geometry. Multi-limb frames
checked limb by limb. Ahegao eyes stay on model.

## Artifacts
Fused fingers, doubled accessories, floating straps, mirrored asymmetry,
seam haze, banded gradients, text burned into the image.

## Continuity
Same seed, sampler and prompt skeleton as the character's other assets. Model
unchanged. Costume state matches the scene's timeline.

## Archive
A replaced asset moves to `rotn-assets/archive/`, never deleted. The registry
row is retired, not removed.

## Sign-off
The venice lane accepts. claude confirms the file loads and the registry
resolves before the asset is referenced in code.
