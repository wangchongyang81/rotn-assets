# 03 — Story CGs

Showcase illustration. Highest priority of any asset class.

## The prop rule
Every realm owns a key prop or motif. Its CG set is built around that prop,
not around a pose. Two realms never share a composition skeleton. The prop is
foreground, midground or the thing being interacted with — never wallpaper.

## Composition
One focal point, off-centre. Foreground layer frames an edge. Acting reads in
the face and the hands. Environment carries the realm, not the background
default. Depth from overlap and haze, not blur.

## Camera
Eye level by default. High and low only when the beat demands it. No dutch
angle without a reason in the script.

## Acting
Emotion in the whole body. Contact points drawn with pressure and deformation.
Cloth reacts at every contact. Fluids follow gravity and surface, never a
decal overlay.

## Resolution
Master 3840x2160. Never upscale a master. Portrait, square and mobile crops
composed at master time; nothing stretched. Delivery ladder 1280, 1920, 2560,
3840 on width.

## Layers
Character, midground and foreground split as separate masters where parallax
or subtle motion is wanted. Sky and background stay one plate.

## Light
Motivated by the scene, matching the realm's key direction. Rim on the near
contact. No bloom on skin.

## Continuity
Face, body, costume, accessories, age read and palette identical to the ref
pack. A CG that drifts is rejected even if it is prettier.

## Format
AVIF primary, WebP fallback. sRGB, 8-bit, quality high enough that a gradient
does not band at 1:1.
