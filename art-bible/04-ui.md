# 04 — UI and presentation

Interface art. Must read as the same world as the CGs.

## Type
Display face for realm and character names, text face for body. Body at 16px
minimum on mobile, 18px on desktop. Line height 1.5. Numerals tabular in
stats and prices. Never letter-space body text.

## Panels
Parchment or slate depending on realm influence, never both in one screen.
One-pixel inner bevel, corner marks on framed panels. Panel opacity floor 0.92
so text holds over any scene.

## Layout split
Mobile: single column, bottom-anchored controls, thumb arcs reachable one-handed.
Desktop: side panels, HUD corners, no element wider than 420px. Ultrawide
anchors to a 21:9 safe centre; the edges carry atmosphere, not controls.

## Icons
Single weight, 24px grid, rounded terminals. One metaphor each, no compound
icons. Disabled is 40 percent opacity plus a desaturate, not colour alone.

## Motion
Panel enter 120ms, exit 90ms. No bounce. Choice hover lifts 1px. CG transition
is a fade, 400ms, no wipe.

## Contrast
Body text at 4.5:1 against its own panel, headings 7:1. State is never carried
by hue alone — shape, weight or icon always accompanies.

## Dialogue
Speaker plate above the box, name in display face. Narration italic, no plate.
Box height fits three lines before scroll. Choice buttons stack, never a radial.

## Gallery
Grid of thumbnails, four across mobile, six desktop. Locked cells show a
silhouette and the realm glyph. Viewer is full-bleed with a bottom bar that
auto-hides.
