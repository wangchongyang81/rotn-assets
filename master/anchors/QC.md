# Anchor acceptance

Gate 2 starts here. An anchor passes only on all seven, and only whole.

## Pass
1. Proportion — seven heads, shoulders two head-widths,
   fingertips reach mid-thigh.
2. Hands — five fingers each, thumb on the inner side, no fusion, no extra.
3. Feet — both planted or deliberately lifted, correct count, no melt.
4. Face — eye line at or below the midpoint of the head, symmetric, jaw reads.
5. Silhouette — the build reads at thumbnail size and matches the variant named
   in the lock.
6. Identity — no drift from the character's own adjectives.
7. Pose — the realm's act reads, and its prop is present or implied.

## Fail
Any of: extra or fused fingers, a hand hidden behind a leg, melted feet,
cropped face or hands, chibi proportion, a grey or studio backdrop, a pose that
could belong to any realm, more than one figure, watermark or text.

## Rule
A fail is a re-render at the same seed with the negative tightened, never a
patch. Three fails on one realm means the pose or the prompt is wrong, not the
seed: stop, amend the pose line, record what changed.

## Approve
Only when the whole anchor passes. Then `approved: true` and the accepted seed
go into the lock. Ground contact and the realm environment are wanted, not fails.
