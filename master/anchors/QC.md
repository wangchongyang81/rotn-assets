# Anchor acceptance

Gate 2 starts here. An anchor passes only on all six, and only whole.

## Pass
1. Proportion — seven and a half to eight heads, shoulders two head-widths,
   fingertips reach mid-thigh.
2. Hands — five fingers each, thumb on the inner side, no fusion, no extra.
3. Feet — both planted and visible, correct count, no melt.
4. Face — eye line at or below the midpoint of the head, symmetric, jaw reads.
5. Silhouette — the build reads at thumbnail size and matches the variant named
   in the lock.
6. Identity — no drift from the variant adjectives.

## Fail
Any of: extra or fused fingers, a hand hidden behind a leg, floating feet,
cropped face or hands, chibi proportion, floor shadow, painted background, more
than one figure, watermark or text.

## Rule
A fail is a re-render at the same seed with the negative tightened, never a
patch. Three fails on one view means the prompt is wrong, not the seed: stop,
amend the prompt, record what changed.

## Approve
Only when the whole anchor passes. Then `approved: true` and the accepted seed
go into the lock. No view is approved alone.
